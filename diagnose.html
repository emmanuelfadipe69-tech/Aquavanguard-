Step 2 · Diagnose

Observation screening

Record visible warning signs and decide what follow-up may be appropriate. This screening does not diagnose water quality.

Observation screening only — not a water-quality test or diagnosis. Clear-looking water can contain germs or chemicals. Never taste water to assess it, and avoid touching water that may contain sewage, fuel, or chemicals.
1 What does the water look like?
2 Is there an unusual smell?
3 Have you seen a nearby pollution source?
4 Has the source or surrounding area flooded recently?
5 Has there been heavy rain near the source in the past 7 days?

Rainfall is self-reported context, not a water test or live forecast. This demo has no real-time weather or El Niño feed.
