Community water action

Our impact

AquaVanguard helps people connect what they observe at a water source with practical next steps. We will report results only when they are supported by evidence.

The problem

Communities in Lagos and Ondo often cannot tell if water is unsafe and do not know what to do next.

[ADD SOURCED STATISTIC AND CITATION]

Who uses it

Community members, schools, community health workers, and local environmental and water officers can use the tools to record observations and find next steps.

How it works

  1. Report an approximate source location and observations.
  2. Diagnose visible signs with a screening checklist.
  3. Act using cautious, practical guidance.
  4. Verify by recording a retest. Reports can also be shared with local officers.

Theory of change

  1. People notice a possible concern and report what they see.
  2. They screen visible signs and review suggested next steps.
  3. They take an appropriate action and record a retest when possible.
  4. Reports can reach local officers, who can follow up on community concerns.

How we will measure success

  • Sources reported
  • Sources screened
  • Retests recorded
  • Improvements indicated (self-reported)
  • Reports shared with officers
  • Officer response time

These are measures to track, not results we are claiming today.

For schools

Students can use the checklist to observe their school's water source with an adult, discuss what the signs can and cannot tell them, and learn careful water handling. They should not taste or handle questionable water.

What it cannot do

AquaVanguard does not test water. A visual checklist or community report is not a laboratory result and cannot replace lab testing or official public-health advice.

Roadmap

Planned areas of work include a shared online database, independent verification partners, more languages, and expansion to more states.

How we are different

Many water apps collect data. AquaVanguard connects what a community sees to what it should do next, works offline, and tracks whether the water improved, while being honest about what it cannot prove.
