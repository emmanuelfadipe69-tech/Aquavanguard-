How it works

1

Report

Pin a polluted river, stream or well and note what you see.

 2

Diagnose

Screen for warning signs and see what follow-up is needed—not a substitute for water testing.

 3

Act

Choose cautious next steps based on the water concern and its possible hazards.

 4

Verify

Retest the water. Only verified improvements earn rewards.

Safety note

AquaVanguard does not replace lab testing. Home filters improve clarity and reduce some contaminants, but they do not guarantee safe water. Always boil or disinfect drinking water after filtering.
