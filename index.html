Safety note

quaVanguard does not replace lab testing. Home filters improve clarity and reduce some contaminants, but they do not guarantee safe water. Use only a validated treatment suited to the suspected hazard and follow local public-health advice. When in doubt, do not drink it untreated. If fuel or chemicals may be present, do not handle, boil, or filter the water.
