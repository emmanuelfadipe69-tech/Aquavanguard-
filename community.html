Compare the same source, test parameter, and method before and after an action. This demo keeps the record on this device and does not declare water safe.

Comparison is not independent verification.

Entered readings are user-provided and not checked against a laboratory report. This prototype has no moderator, community identity checks, or server. It therefore keeps every retest pending, awards no points, and never turns a pin green based on a self-reported entry.

What unlocks verified rewards later?

A real deployment needs an independent reviewer or a traceable test provider to check the source, method, and comparable results. Only then could a verified improvement earn a badge or points. A lower reading for one indicator never proves water is safe overall.
